Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

StoryArc is a free practice tool for telling stories out loud. This page explains what it keeps about you, what it sends to other services, and how to have your data removed.

What StoryArc stores about you

When you sign in with Google, StoryArc stores:

  • your email address,
  • your Google account ID,
  • how much of your practice time you have used, and how many analyses you have run.

That is all. It is used only to apply the per-account usage limit, and it is never sold or shared. StoryArc does not use advertising or tracking cookies; the only cookie StoryArc itself sets keeps you signed in.

Your recordings and stories

  • Your recording is sent to Deepgram to be turned into text, with Deepgram's model-improvement programme switched off for these requests.
  • That text is sent to Google's Gemini API for the feedback you see.
  • StoryArc's servers keep neither the recording nor the text once your feedback is returned.
  • Your practice history (transcripts and feedback) is saved only in your own browser. You can export it or delete it at any time from the History page.

What we receive from Google

Google sign-in shares your name, email address, profile picture and account ID with StoryArc. StoryArc keeps only the email address and account ID, as listed above.

Deleting your data

Email amalsurender1234@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and your account record will be deleted. Your practice history lives in your browser; clear it from the History page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
